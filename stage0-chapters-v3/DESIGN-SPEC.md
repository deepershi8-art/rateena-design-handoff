# RATEENA SOUND: Stage 00 Chapter Screens, Design Spec (v3)

All values are taken from the shipped CSS in `stage00-chNN-{en,ar}.html` (generated by `build_v3.py`).
`clamp(min, fluid, max)` values are given with their resolved value at 390 px / 834 px / 1440 px.

## 1. Frames & breakpoints

| Device | CSS viewport | Export @2x | Export @3x |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mobile (reference) | 390 × 844 | 780 × 1688 | 1170 × 2532 |
| Tablet | 834 × 1112 | 1668 × 2224 | n/a |
| Desktop | 1440 × 900 | 2880 × 1800 | n/a |

Breakpoints used in CSS:
- `max-width: 540px`: tables become stacked cards (chapter 4 facilities table).
- `min-width: 600px`: strip items widen 80 → 88 px.
- `min-width: 900px`: banner is no longer full width; it is centred at its own aspect ratio, max height `min(26vh, 360px)`.

The screen is a fixed full-viewport app (`position:fixed; inset:0`), with a CSS grid of 5 rows:
`header (auto) · banner (auto) · stage line (auto) · card (1fr, scrolls internally) · strip nav (auto)`.
The page itself never scrolls; only the card body scrolls.

Safe areas: when the app is in the native shell, add `env(safe-area-inset-top)` to the header top padding and `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` to the nav bottom padding (iPhone: 47 / 34 pt). Keep the 390 × 844 layout inside those insets.

## 6. Banners (chapter headers)

All 10 banners were rebuilt to one standard, following the chapter 7 banner as the master for the text block. Each banner has the authentic `rateena-logo.svg` top-left, then **STAGE 00**, **FRAMING AND ASSUMPTIONS**, a short gold rule, **CHAPTER NN** (correct number) and the chapter title in Montserrat light caps, gold. The artwork on the right is the owner's, untouched. Banners are **never cropped and never mirrored in Arabic**.

- Size: **1920 × 1080 px, 16 : 9**, identical for all 10.
- Shown at 390 px wide: **390 × 219 px** (capped by `max-height:min(32vh,440px)`; at 844 px tall the cap is 270 px, so it shows full width).
- CSS: `width:100%; height:auto; max-height:min(32vh,440px); object-fit:contain`; on ≥ 900 px `width:auto; max-height:min(26vh,360px)`, centred on black.
- Text block on the 1920 × 1080 master: logo x 128, y 52, 219 px wide; text column starts x 137; STAGE 00 at y 300 (34 px, 600, tracking .2em); FRAMING AND ASSUMPTIONS 23 px (400, tracking .16em); rule 88 × 4 px gold; CHAPTER NN 31 px (300, tracking .32em); title 50 to 80 px (300, line-height 1.0). ## 7. Behaviour

- Chapters switch with a cross-fade on the card: opacity 0 → 1 over 0.4 s ease, with a 280 ms fade-out before navigation. This respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Strip: horizontal scroll with snap (`x proximity`), no scrollbar; the active item is gold at full opacity with a 1 px gold underline and is centred on load. Inactive items are at 45 % opacity.
- Counter "NN / 10" is always LTR, including in Arabic (`direction:ltr; unicode-bidi:isolate`).
- AR pages use `dir="rtl"`: header, stage line, strip order and text alignment are mirrored, and the back chevron is flipped. Banners and the logo are **not** mirrored.
- The EN ⇄ عربي switch goes to the same chapter in the other language.

## 8. Writing rule

No long dashes anywhere in the app (no em dash, no en dash, no spaced hyphen used as a dash). Use a comma, a full stop, a colon or a line break instead (Arabic: ، or a full stop). Hyphens inside words (Room-within-a-Room, world-class) are fine.
