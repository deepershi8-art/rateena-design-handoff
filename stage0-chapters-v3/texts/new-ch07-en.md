## Chapter 7: How Will We Tell This Story?

Studio design has its own language. It's full of ratings, curves, acronyms, and words borrowed from physics, engineering and construction.

Some of those words are precise and mean something important. But plenty of them get used just to make someone sound scientific, even when the listener has no idea what they actually mean.

So in this study, every time a new technical term comes up, we'll explain it the first time in plain, simple words. We'll also gather them in a Glossary that grows as we move through each part of the study.

When we give numbers, measurements or standards, we won't just drop them on the page. We'll say what the number means, why it matters, and what it does to the studio we're building.

We'd rather be a little too clear than leave you nodding along to words you only half understand.

The voice here is someone who's done the work, and lived recording and production sessions from inside the studio.

This isn't a sales brochure for the project. And we won't turn it into a wall of specs and standards that nobody explains.

When we pick one design approach over another, we'll tell you why, and what the technical reasons are.

We want investors to understand our decisions, talk them through with us, ask sharp questions, and push back when they've got a good reason.

We don't want anyone signing off on decisions worth millions just because they heard impressive engineering words they didn't follow.
