## Chapter 8: The Road Ahead. The Parts of Phase One

This study moves forward one part at a time, and each part builds on what we learned and decided in the one before.

We'll start with the size of the rooms, and why floor area and height matter so much to how they sound.

Then we'll talk about how to shut those rooms off from the noise of the city and the vibration around them.

Once we're sure outside sound isn't getting in, we'll turn to the sound inside the rooms, and how to make it behave.

After that comes interior design, furniture and looks: how to make a studio feel luxurious and comfortable without giving up anything acoustically.

Then we get to power, grounding, cabling, and the infrastructure that carries the audio, so it all runs without noise or electrical interference.

Here's the order of the parts:

### Part One: Stage 01
**The Size of the Flagship Studio: How Much Space and Height Do We Need, and Why?**
We'll look at the size of the rooms, especially the Live Room, and the technical reasons behind the floor area and height they need.

### Part Two: Stage 02
**Isolation**
How we keep the outside world out of the studio, and the music in.
We'll look at how sound and vibration travel, the different ways to isolate, and how we stop sound crossing from one room to another.

### Part Three: Stage 03
**Room Acoustics**
Once the room is isolated, how do we make the sound inside it honest, balanced, beautiful, and usable for recording and mixing?

### Part Four: Stage 04
**Interior Design, Furniture, and Quiet Luxury**
How do we design a studio that is comfortable, beautiful and luxurious, without the interior design conflicting with the requirements of isolation and acoustic treatment?

### Part Five: Stage 05
**Power, Grounding, Cabling, and Signal Infrastructure**
How do we bring in power and route the cables and audio signals reliably, without letting hum or electrical noise into the recordings?

### Part Six: Stage 06
**Equipment Classes and the Signal Path**
We'll go through the classes of equipment we need, and follow the audio signal from the moment sound hits the microphone, through recording, processing and mixing, all the way to the final master.

### Part Seven: Stage 07
**The Remaining Facilities of the Audio Center**
We'll give short technical outlines for the rehearsal hall, the Foley studio, the voice, spoken-word and Quran rooms, and the three music studios.
We'll come back to the full technical detail for these in Phase Two.

### Part Eight: Stage 08
**Study Fees and the Shape of Phase Two**
We'll set out the fees for the technical study, outline Phase Two and the work it involves, and explain how we'll carry on with the rest of the project's facilities at the same technical depth.

And one thing should be clear from the start:

**Phase One is a purely technical study.**

There are no construction costs or equipment prices in it. That money conversation gets its own stage, once we've pinned down exactly what's needed technically.
