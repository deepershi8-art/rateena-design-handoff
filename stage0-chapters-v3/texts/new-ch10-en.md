## Chapter 10: The Next Step. The Size of a Room

So now we know what we're protecting.

A singer's whisper at seven in the evening, right in the middle of Cairo's noise.

A drum kit that sounds as big, powerful and present as it really is.

And a control room that tells us the truth, so the mix we make there holds up wherever it's played.

But the first technical decision that shapes all of this is also one of the first to get squeezed once the architect starts drawing.

That decision is the size of the room.

Before we talk about walls, isolation, acoustic treatment, speakers or cables, we have to answer a question that sounds simple, but isn't:

Exactly how big does the world-class flagship studio need to be? And why?

That's the question Part One of the study starts with.
