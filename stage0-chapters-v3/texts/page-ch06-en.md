## The Building: What Do We Need From It?

Finding the right land or building, negotiating the lease or purchase, and making the final choice of site are all down to the client.

Our job is to spell out the technical requirements the building has to meet, so whoever is finding or planning it knows exactly what to look for.

We'll also help with choosing the location, because in a city like Cairo the wrong site can ruin even the best studio design before anyone lays a brick.
### A Separate Location for the World-Class Flagship Studio

Putting the Flagship Studio somewhere separate from the rest of the integrated audio center is a core part of our vision.

It comes down to who we expect to welcome: artists, producers and high-profile guests. They need real calm, discretion and complete privacy for their whole visit and every session.

That's why we see a dedicated, independent site for the flagship studio as essential. It's the only way to give our guests the quiet, private place their work needs.
### Siting Guidance for Cairo (Suggestions Only)

Cairo is a noisy city.

But here's the surprising part: the real enemy isn't always the noise we hear. Sometimes it's the vibration we feel.

Car horns, traffic and voices travel through the air. Well-designed isolating walls and properly sealed construction can cut them down.

But stand near a metro line and you might feel the train under your feet before you hear it.

Those low-frequency vibrations travel through the ground, then climb up through the structure of the building above. Stopping them is a lot harder than stopping noise in the air.

The simplest fix is to stay away from sites near big sources of ground vibration in the first place.

We'll cover the other isolation and vibration-control techniques later in the study.
